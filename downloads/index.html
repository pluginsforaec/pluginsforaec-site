Compatibility

  • Everything works on SketchUp 2023 and later; tested every day on SketchUp 2025 for Windows.
  • On SketchUp 2021 and 2022 the tools work; the Gumball and the Inspector card need 2023 and do not appear.
  • One installation holds every tool you own; new tools join the same toolbar.
