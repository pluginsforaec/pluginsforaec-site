Divide+

A tool with a live preview: type 3 (parts), 3x2 (grid) or 600mm (spacing) and press Enter. Edges keep their data; a whole curve is divided along its length and keeps its shape (an arc at equal angles). A face grid runs along the edge you click (else the longest, said on the card), every cell is made and a solid stays a solid. Closed groups are never entered; one Undo.

How to use it

  1. Select edges, whole curves (all their edges) or faces.
  2. Start Divide+ (toolbar, or Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp › GEOMETRY); the division is previewed and the card shows what Enter will do.
  3. Type a count, a grid or a spacing and press Enter, or just press Enter for what the card shows.
  4. Use Up / Down (or − / + on the card) for parts; on a face, Right / Left for parts across.
  5. On a face, click one of its edges to run the grid along it; otherwise it follows the longest edge.
  6. With nothing selected, click edges or faces to pick them, then press Enter.

Keys

Up / Down
one part more / one part less
Right / Left
one part more / less across a selected face
Tab
start the spacing from the other end (only when a spacing is typed)
Enter
divide as shown
Esc
leave the tool

Type in the measurements box

3
3 equal parts (on a face: 3 along the grid direction)
3x2
a face grid 3 along by 2 across; edges and curves take 3 parts
600mm
a division every 600 mm from the start; on a face, grid lines every 600 mm both ways; the card shows the last part
0.6m
the same spacing typed in metres

Good to know

  • An edge keeps its tag, softening, material and visibility on every part.
  • A whole curve (arc, circle, welded curve) is divided along its length and stays one curve with its shape; an arc at equal angles.
  • A face grid takes equal steps of the face's bounding box, is clipped to the outline and holes, and makes every cell; a solid stays a solid.
  • Up to 500 parts. Above about 5,000 cells the tool asks before dividing.
  • Items shorter than one part are left whole. Text, dimensions and guides are left alone.
  • Closed groups and components are not entered: open them first. The counts are remembered; one Undo removes the division.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
