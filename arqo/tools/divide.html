Divide+

A tool with a live preview: type 3 (parts), 3x2 (grid) or 600mm (spacing) and press Enter. Edges keep their data; a whole curve is divided along its length and keeps its shape (an arc at equal angles). A face grid runs along the edge you click (else the longest, said on the card), every cell is made and a solid stays a solid. Closed groups are never entered; one Undo.
