ARQOPush

One Push/Pull for everything, in twelve modes: Push/Pull, Multi Face, Joint Push, Push to Face, Push to Object, Thicken, Taper, Offset + Push, Copy + Push, Symmetric, Axis and Repeat. It also pushes a face inside a closed group without opening it, keeps the paint of the faces it continues, and can step the distance by 10, 50 or 100 mm.

BeforeAfter

How to use it

  1. Click ARQOPush and move over a face: the push follows the mouse.
  2. Click to set it, or type a distance and press Enter (700).
  3. Pick a mode on the card: Multi Face pushes every selected face by the same distance; Axis pushes along X, Y or Z; Repeat pushes each face you click by the last distance.
  4. Joint Push and Thicken keep curved and joined surfaces in one piece, evenly thick.
  5. One Undo takes it back.

Good to know

  • Push/Pull takes one face or joined faces in one plane; for separate faces use Multi Face.
  • Faces meeting at an angle are refused by the flat modes: Joint Push and Thicken keep them joined and equally thick.
  • Joint Push and Thicken refuse a distance that would fold a curved surface through itself.
  • Thicken makes a closed shell; Symmetric grows the face the distance each way; Copy + Push leaves the face and pushes a copy.
  • Offset + Push takes one face without holes; an offset that does not fit is refused.
  • Push to Face: a slanted target slopes the top onto it; a face parallel to the push is refused.
  • Axis: an axis lying in the face, or a slanted push into the body, is refused.
  • Push/Pull and Axis stay on after a push: click the next face, or type a distance to change the last one.
  • A face inside a closed group is pushed without opening it; locked groups are left alone; a shared component asks first.
  • New faces take the paint of the face they continue. Step on the card snaps the distance to 10, 50 or 100 mm.
  • Every push is one Undo; a refused push changes nothing.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
