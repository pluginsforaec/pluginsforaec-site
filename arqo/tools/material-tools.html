Material Tools

Replace every use of one material with another (or Default) in the selection or the whole model, nested groups and components included; swap front and back materials; turn faces so their front side faces out, keeping the paint where it was.

How to use it

  1. Open Material Tools; choose In selection or Whole model.
  2. In Replace, choose the material (or select something and press Pick to take its material).
  3. In With, choose the new material, or Default.
  4. Tick Back faces too or Remove the old material if needed, then press Replace.
  5. For the selected faces and everything inside selected groups, press Swap front / back or Turn faces outward.

Good to know

  • Replace changes face fronts, backs (when ticked), edges, groups and components, nested ones included.
  • The Replace list shows the materials used in the chosen scope, with how many places use each.
  • Remove the old material works only with Whole model, and only when nothing still uses it (locked containers keep it).
  • Turn faces outward makes joined faces consistent; a closed mesh faces out, an open one faces up. The paint stays on its side.
  • In a shared component only the selected copy changes; the panel shows 1/4 first and you may choose every copy. Whole model reaches every copy.
  • Locked groups and components are not entered and are counted. Each action is one Undo.
  • Swap front / back and Turn faces outward are also in Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp › MATERIALS; Turn faces outward is in the right-click ARQO menu when a group or component is selected.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
