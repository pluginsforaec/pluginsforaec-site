Kitchen Builder

Click where the run starts and where it ends (or type its length): base units, a sink with its basin and tap, a dishwasher, a hob with the oven under it and a hood over it, drawer units, wall units and, if you like, a tall fridge — filled by rule, each a named group. Choose the fronts (flat, shaker, fluted, handleless), the handles (bar, knob, edge pull, none) and the worktop (stone, oak, concrete); Tab puts the units on the other side of the line. One group on the tag ARQO Kitchen, one Undo.

How to use it

  1. Start Kitchen Builder; on the card choose the layout (base, base and wall units, with a tall fridge), the fronts, the handles and the worktop.
  2. Click where the run starts, on the floor at the wall.
  3. Click where it ends, or move the mouse the way it goes and type its length; the card lists the units it will hold.
  4. Press Tab if the units should stand on the other side of the line.

Good to know

  • The run is filled by rule: a sink (800) near the middle, a dishwasher beside it, a hob with the oven under it a unit away, 600 drawer units, then 450, 400, 300, and a filler for what is left.
  • Units: 18 carcasses on a 100 plinth set back; worktop 40, 910 high, overhanging 20; wall units 720 high from 1450, 350 deep; a hood over the hob; a tall fridge housing 2150 at the right end.
  • Fronts: flat, shaker, fluted oak or handleless; handles: bar, knob, edge pull or none; worktop: stone, oak or concrete — all ARQO's own finishes.
  • One group on the tag ARQO Kitchen, a named group for each unit; one Undo takes it back. Choices are remembered.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
