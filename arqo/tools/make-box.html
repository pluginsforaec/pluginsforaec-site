Make Box+

Two corners along a side, a third for the width (on the floor, a wall or a slope), then the height — clicked or typed; the mouse is followed square to the base and only points it lands on set the height. A closed box in its own group, one Undo.

How to use it

  1. Start Make Box+ and click the first corner of the base.
  2. Click the second corner along one side.
  3. Click the base's width; it is read square to that side, in the plane of the three points.
  4. Move to the height and click, or type the height and press Enter.

Keys

Shift (hold)
From the second click on: locks the direction
Arrows
From the second click on: lock the direction to an axis
Enter
After the base: builds at the typed height
Esc
Drops the clicks made so far, then leaves

Type in the measurements box

600
After the base: a 600 high box
-600
After the base: the box goes below the base

Good to know

  • The height follows the mouse square to the base; only a point the mouse lands on (an endpoint, a midpoint) sets it exactly.
  • The card shows width × depth × height while you draw.
  • The group is named with its sizes, for example Box 1200 × 600 × 600.
  • A zero height is refused and nothing is drawn.
  • Drawn in the group or component that is open. One Undo takes it back.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
