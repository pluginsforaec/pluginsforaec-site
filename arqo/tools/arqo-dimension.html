Dimension

Select what to dimension and run Dimension: each edge gets its length (aligned with it, or only its X or Y run), set off away from what it bounds; each arc its radius and each circle its diameter; any other curve its length written once beside it; each face its area in m² at its middle; each group or component its overall X and Y along its front and right side. With two edges meeting and the angle chosen, the angle between them is drawn as an arc and its degrees. Everything goes on the tag ARQO Dimensions, set off by the distance typed. One Undo.

Pictures and a short film of Dimension at work come here.

How to use it

  1. Select edges, arcs, circles, faces, groups or components.
  2. Click Dimension.
  3. In the box: what to dimension (auto, only X, only Y, or the angle of two edges) and how far to set it off.
  4. The dimensions are made on the tag ARQO Dimensions; the message says how many of each.

Good to know

  • A curve gets one label for its whole length, not one per segment.
  • Hide the tag ARQO Dimensions to hide them all.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
