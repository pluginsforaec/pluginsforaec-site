Smart Slice

Slice, cut or detach groups and components with a plane from two or three points, a face, an edge (perpendicular) or a typed height; the cut is closed with a section face.

How to use it

  1. Select groups, components or loose faces and edges, then start Smart Slice.
  2. Press Tab to choose Slice (two parts), Cut (remove one side) or Detach (move one part away).
  3. Click two points for a vertical cut, or type a height for a level cut.
  4. For other planes, press Alt for three points, or choose Face / edge on the card and click one.
  5. Cut: click the side to remove. Detach: press Enter to move the part shown, or click the other part.

Keys

Tab
Next mode: Slice, Cut, Detach
Alt
Switch between two points and three points
Shift
After the first click: hold to keep the direction
Arrows
After the first click: lock to an axis
Enter
Detach: move the default part (toward +X, +Y or up)
Esc
Drop the plane; again to leave the tool

Type in the measurements box

1200mm
A level cut at a height of 1200 mm

Good to know

  • A face gives its own plane; an edge gives the plane square to it through the click.
  • A section face is made only where the object is a closed solid; open objects are cut without one.
  • Objects inside that cross the cut are cut too; text, dimensions and guides stay with their side.
  • The parts keep the object's settings and attributes. A shared component: only this copy changes, and the report says so.
  • A locked object across the cut, or an item that cannot be cut, refuses the whole cut. Locked objects in the selection are skipped and counted.
  • Detach moves the part 300 mm by default. The mode is remembered. One Undo removes it.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
