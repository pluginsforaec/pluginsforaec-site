Curve to Arc

Chains of short edges that lie on a circle, as arcs come in from CAD, become SketchUp arcs and circles with a centre and a radius again, same segments, faces rebuilt. Chains off a circle are left alone.

How to use it

  1. Select the edges of the curve, or a face to use its edges; one edge of a curve brings the whole curve.
  2. Click Curve to Arc (toolbar, or Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp › DOCTOR).
  3. Read the message: arcs and circles made, the tolerance used, and what was left and why.
  4. If chains were left, raise the tolerance in Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp › Settings › Curve to Arc… and run it again.

Good to know

  • Tolerance: Deviation from the circle, 1 mm by default. Vertices are moved onto the arc by at most this much; a run that needs more is left, and the message says how much it needed.
  • The arc is drawn over the existing edges: the same edges and faces stay, with their materials, tags, attributes and softening, and a solid stays a solid. Nothing is erased.
  • An arc needs at least three edges on one circle. Straight edges in an outline (line, arc, line) stay straight; single edges are ignored.
  • Left alone: chains that are SketchUp arcs already, arcs that are part of a curve running past them (explode that curve first), and runs with single edges drawn the other way.
  • If SketchUp would add an edge or a face, the whole command is rolled back and the model does not change.
  • Closed groups and components are not entered: double-click to open them first. One Undo.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
