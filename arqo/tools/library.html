ARQO Library

The Library opens on a tab a tool — doors and windows first, walls, stairs, kitchens and roofs as they join. On the left the catalogue: families and types with pictures from our own SketchUp, search and favourites. On the right the form: every dimension a field with its unit, the lining, architrave, leaf, handle height and hinges of a door, the frame, sash, glazing unit and sills of a window, the opening angle and hand. Select a placed element and the form shows its values; each change rebuilds it at once (one Undo each). With nothing selected, Place starts the tool with the form's values. Presets by name; the rules of practice read back as checks.

How to use it

  1. Open the Library; choose the tab: Walls, Doors, Windows, Stairs, Kitchens or Roofs.
  2. Pick a family and a type from the pictures; search by name; star your favourites.
  3. Set the sizes and details in the form: each value in the model's units, within its range; the checks below say what the rules of practice expect.
  4. Press Place: Smart Door / Window starts with these values. Or select a placed element first: the form shows it and every change rebuilds it at once.
  5. Save the form as a preset by name; load or delete presets from the list.
  6. Kitchens: select a kitchen drawn by Kitchen Builder. Its runs show as strips of units to scale: click a unit to change its kind or width, move it with the arrows or drag it along the run, duplicate or delete it, or add a unit. The filler takes what is left; a change that would not fit is refused and says by how much.
  7. Pick fronts, handles and worktops from the pictures; layout, island, front colour, hood, splashback and heights are on the form. With no kitchen selected, Draw a kitchen starts Kitchen Builder with these settings.

Good to know

  • Doors: width, height, hand, opening angle, lining, architrave width and depth, leaf thickness, handle height, hinges (auto: two up to 985 × 2110, three beyond).
  • Windows: width, height, sill height, hand, angle, frame face and depth, sash face, glazing unit (4-16-4, 4-12-4-12-4 …), sill and window board projections.
  • Editing a placed opening draws its wall again from the wall's data and cuts every opening in it again; one Undo a change.
  • The pictures are made from our own SketchUp; everything in the Library is ARQO's own style.
  • Kitchen checks: the dishwasher beside the sink, the hob away from tall units, at least 600 of worktop between the sink and the hob, worktop height, base depth and the gap under the wall units. Every kitchen change is one Undo; an Undo outside the panel is shown on the strip at once.
  • Walls, stairs and roofs: select one and the form shows its values; a picture or a field draws it again at once (one Undo). A wall keeps its doors and windows, cut again; a stair drawn with Smart Stair 0.4 or later can be changed here.
  • Furniture (Tefriş): bedroom, living, dining, study and bath pieces with pictures; each size a field in cm, the wood and the fabric chosen. Place hands it to SketchUp's own placing (it sits on the floor); a selected piece is drawn again in place. Lengths in the Library are in cm; type 25mm or 0.9m to give another unit.
  • Tiles: pick a pattern, size and finish by picture, select a face and press Lay on the selected face; select laid tiles to change them in place (one Undo), their area and tile count on the form.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
