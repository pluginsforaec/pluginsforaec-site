Smart Railing

Click along the edge: each click a corner, Enter or a double click ends it, a click on the first point closes a ring round a void. Tab picks the infill — bars, glass, rods, solid upstand, timber spindles, a patterned panel, a sculpted balustrade or a lamella screen — the same as ARQO Stair+, built so a 10 cm sphere does not pass. Type the height (1100 by default; under 900 refused, under 1100 the card warns). Select a Smart Railing first and the tool changes its infill or height in place. Each run is a group on the tag ARQO Railings with its path, infill and height written on it. One Undo.

Pictures and a short film of Smart Railing at work come here.

How to use it

  1. Click Smart Railing (toolbar, or Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp).
  2. Tab picks the infill; type the height and press Enter (1100 by default).
  3. Click along the edge: each click a corner.
  4. Enter or a double click ends it; a click on the first point closes a ring.
  5. To change one: select it, start Smart Railing, press Tab or type a height.
