Tools · Materials

Paint+

Paint every face inside selected groups and components (backs and unpainted faces as options), clear materials, flip faces, rotate, mirror or align textures to an edge, and measure the area each material covers in the selection.

How to use it

  1. Select faces, groups or components, and open Paint+.
  2. Choose a material (the one current in SketchUp's Materials tray comes preselected).
  3. Tick Back faces too or Only unpainted faces if needed, then press Paint every face inside.
  4. To clear, tick Fronts, Backs and Groups / components as needed, then press Clear material; Flip faces reverses faces.
  5. For textured faces press ↺ 90°, ↻ 90°, 180°, Mirror ↔ or Mirror ↕; Align to edge needs one selected edge.
  6. Press Measure selection for the area of each material.

Good to know

  • Works on the selection and inside selected groups and components, nested ones included.
  • Flip faces turns the paint with the face, like SketchUp's Reverse Faces.
  • Texture turns and mirrors are about the face's first corner; scale and position are kept. Faces without a texture are left alone.
  • Measure selection lists front-face area per material, Default included, in model units; nothing changes.
  • In a shared component only the selected copy changes; the panel shows 1/4 first and you may choose every copy.
  • Locked groups and components are kept and counted. Each action is one Undo.
  • Flip faces and Clear material are also in Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp › MATERIALS; Flip faces is in the right-click ARQO menu when a group or component is selected.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
