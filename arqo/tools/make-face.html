Make Face+

Create faces from selected edges, also from the right-click menu.

How to use it

  1. Select the edges that bound the areas (a selected face gives its edges).
  2. Run Make Face+.
  3. Read the message: how many faces were made and what was left, and why.

Good to know

  • Networks work: two rooms sharing a wall, a grid, a square with a diagonal, a wireframe box.
  • Edges that are not selected are never used.
  • A new horizontal face looks up.
  • A loop inside another becomes its own face, and the outer face gets a hole.
  • Loops that already have a face are left as they are; loops not in one plane are left open.
  • Edges crossing without a vertex bound no face by default; choose Split at crossings in Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp › Settings › Make Face+ to split them.
  • Edges inside an open group get their face there; closed groups and components are not entered.
  • It only adds faces. One Undo removes them.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
