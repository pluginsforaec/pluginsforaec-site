Sun & View

Select a window face or a Smart Door / Window opening and run Sun & View. Sun: the hours of direct sun on it on the 21st of every month, from the model's geo-location (latitude, longitude, time zone, north angle) — every half hour the sun's direction, counted when it is up, in front of the window and not hidden by anything in the model; a table beside the model and the year in words. View: a fan of rays ±60° across the window, each stopping at the first thing or at 50 m, drawn on the tag ARQO Sun & View, and how much of it is open. The sun part changes nothing; the fan is one Undo.

Pictures and a short film of Sun & View at work come here.

How to use it

  1. Set the model's place in Model Info › Geo-location.
  2. Select a window face or an opening.
  3. Click Sun & View; in the box: sun, view or both.
  4. The message gives the hours; the table is beside the model; the fan is drawn on its tag.

Good to know

  • Anything in the model in the way shades the window.
  • Hide the tag ARQO Sun & View to hide the fan.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
