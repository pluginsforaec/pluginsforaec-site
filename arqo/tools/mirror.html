Mirror+

Mirror across two clicked points, a clicked face (Tab) or the plane through the selection's centre square to X, Y or Z (arrows or the card); as a copy that keeps every setting and attribute, or in place (Ctrl).

Pictures and a short film of Mirror+ at work come here.

How to use it

  1. Select groups, components, faces or edges, and start Mirror+.
  2. Press Ctrl to choose copy or in place.
  3. Click two points: the mirror is the vertical plane through them.
  4. Or press Tab and click a face: its plane is the mirror.
  5. Or, before the first click, press an arrow for the plane through the selection's centre, then Enter or click.

Keys

Ctrl
Copy ↔ in place (remembered)
Tab
Mirror across a clicked face; Tab again goes back to two points
→ ← ↑
Before the first click: the plane square to X / Y / Z through the selection's centre; the same arrow again lets it go
Shift (hold)
After the first click: locks the direction
Arrows
After the first click: lock the direction to an axis
Enter
Mirrors across the axis plane shown
Esc
Lets the axis plane go, then the clicks, then leaves

Good to know

  • The preview shows the selection's real edges, mirrored.
  • On the card, Axes chooses World or Object: the selected object's own axes, as the Gumball uses them.
  • A copy carries its source's data: attributes, dynamic component settings, front and back materials, texture positions, curves, guides, text and dimensions.
  • What cannot be carried is named on the card first; a copy that would lose data, or would lie off-register on existing faces, is refused and nothing changes.
  • Locked groups and components are skipped and counted.
  • Inside an open component placed several times, the copies it affects are shown first; Cancel changes nothing.
  • One Undo removes it.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
