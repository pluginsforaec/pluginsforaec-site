Attach to Face

Enter drops every selected group and component along an axis until it meets the first face below it (terrain, floors); a click on a face places them there keeping their offsets; Shift stands them square to the surface. Previewed as ghosts, one undo step.

How to use it

  1. Select groups, components or loose geometry, then start Attach to Face (Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp).
  2. Choose the travel axis with the arrows; blue Z is the default.
  3. Press Enter: each object travels along the axis, down or up, until it touches the nearest face.
  4. Or hover a face to preview and click it: the objects land there and keep their offsets.
  5. Press Shift (or use the card) to stand the objects square to the surface.

Keys

→ ← ↑
travel axis: X red, Y green, Z blue
Shift
square to the surface / keep orientation (switches)
Enter
set every object down along the axis
Esc
leave the tool

Good to know

  • On a drop, objects that meet no face, or already sit on one, stay where they are and are counted.
  • On a click, the object nearest the click lands on it: on a floor its base, on a wall the side facing it.
  • Square to the surface turns the object so its blue axis follows the face normal.
  • The objects' own faces never stop them; sizes come from the geometry, not from text or dimensions.
  • Loose faces and edges joined together move as one body.
  • Locked objects are left as they are. One Undo removes the move.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
