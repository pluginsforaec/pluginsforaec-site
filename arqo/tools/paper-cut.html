Paper Cut

Select groups or components and run Paper Cut: each solid's faces are opened out across their shared edges into flat nets, none stretched and none lying on another (a face that would starts a new piece). Cut lines solid, fold lines dashed, a flat join not drawn; a glue tab on one side of every glued edge, both sides numbered alike; each piece numbered. At 1:20, 1:50, 1:100 or 1:200 on A4 sheets, in one PDF beside the model; a piece larger than the sheet is refused with the scale that fits. Nothing in the model changes.

Pictures and a short film of Paper Cut at work come here.

How to use it

  1. Select groups or components (solids).
  2. Click Paper Cut.
  3. In the box: the scale (1:20, 1:50, 1:100, 1:200).
  4. The PDF is written beside the model: cut on the solid lines, fold on the dashed ones, glue the tabs to the edge with the same number.

Good to know

  • A face that would lie on another starts a new piece.
  • If a piece is larger than A4, ARQO says which scale fits.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
