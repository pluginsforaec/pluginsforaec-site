Multi Slice

Planes square to X, Y or Z (arrow keys): click the first plane and the spacing, or type x4 for equal parts or 300mm for a spacing; slice or detach; every plane previewed, the pieces of one plane cut by the next. On Smart Slice's engine.

How to use it

  1. Select groups, components or loose geometry, and start Multi Slice.
  2. Before the first click, choose the axis with an arrow (the planes are square to it; Z by default).
  3. Press Tab to choose Slice or Detach.
  4. Click where the first plane goes, then a point at the spacing; or type x4 or 300mm and press Enter.

Keys

→
Before the first click: planes square to X
←
Before the first click: planes square to Y
↑
Before the first click: planes square to Z
Tab
Slice / Detach
Esc
Drops the clicks made so far, then leaves

Type in the measurements box

x4
Four equal parts across the selection
300mm
A plane every 300 mm, from the first click or the low end

Good to know

  • After the first click the arrows change nothing.
  • The planes repeat across the whole selection; equal parts and spacing follow the geometry, not text, dimensions or guides.
  • Detach moves each plane's lower piece down by the gap (300 mm unless changed in Settings), with the pieces below it.
  • Loose geometry is cut and given back loose; your groups are never exploded.
  • Locked selected objects are skipped and counted. A locked object across a cut, or something that cannot be cut safely, refuses the whole cut: nothing changes.
  • One Undo takes the whole cut back.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
