Smart Stair

Click the foot and the top: the risers, the going and 2R+G come from the levels, checked against the dwelling-stair rules (riser at most 180, going at least 270, Blondel 600–650). Simple, Slab, ZigZag, Steps and Treads; a typed width or number of risers; one group, one Undo.

How to use it

  1. Start Smart Stair and press Tab to choose Simple, Slab, ZigZag, Steps or Treads.
  2. Type a width, or a number of risers, if you need them.
  3. Click the foot: where the first riser stands.
  4. Move the mouse; the card shows risers, going, 2R+G and the rule checks.
  5. Click the top: the edge the stair arrives at.

Good to know

  • The number of risers comes from a 175 mm riser unless you type it; the last step ends level with the top edge.
  • Checks: riser at most 180 mm, going at least 270 mm, 2R+G between 600 and 650 mm. A stair that fails is still drawn and the report says why.
  • The top must be higher than the foot. Clicked straight above the foot, the going is 280 mm.
  • Simple, Slab and ZigZag are one closed solid; Steps and Treads are one body per step inside the stair's group.
  • Type and width are remembered. One group, drawn in the open context; one Undo removes it.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
