Draw Boundary

Draw a boundary from a coordinate file.

How to use it

  1. Click Draw Boundary (toolbar, or Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp › DATA).
  2. Choose the coordinate file (.csv, .txt or .xyz).
  3. In the box, set the file's unit (m, cm, mm), the column order (x, y or y, x) and where it goes.
  4. Confirm: the points are joined in file order into a closed boundary.
  5. Read the message: points used, the face area, the shift to the origin and any skipped lines.

Good to know

  • A line can be x, y / x, y, z / name, x, y (, z) / number, x, y, z, split by commas, semicolons, tabs or spaces. With semicolons, tabs or spaces a comma is read as the decimal mark (1000,5;2000,25).
  • Lines that are not points, such as a header, are skipped and counted. At least three points are needed.
  • First point at the model's origin (the default) shifts the boundary in x and y only; heights are kept and the shift is written on the group. Or keep the real coordinates.
  • A face is made only when the points lie in one plane (within 1 mm), and its area is given; otherwise only the edges are drawn.
  • Repeated points in a row, and a last point that repeats the first, are dropped.
  • The three choices are remembered. Drawn in the open context; one Undo.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
