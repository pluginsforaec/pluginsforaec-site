Hide / Show Lines

One click hides the seam lines of the selection (lines between two faces lying in one plane: where objects were intersected or joined), or shows hidden edges again. Also: hide every edge. Works inside groups and components (only the selected copies of a shared component unless you choose all). Hidden, never erased; one Undo.

How to use it

  1. Select geometry, groups or components.
  2. Click the toolbar button: it hides the seam lines, or, when there are none, shows the hidden edges again.
  3. Or right-click › ARQO (also Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp › DOCTOR) and choose hide seam lines, hide every edge or show hidden edges.
  4. If a component is placed more than once, choose Selected only or All copies when asked.
  5. Read the message: how many lines were hidden or shown.

Good to know

  • A seam is a visible, unsoftened edge between two faces lying in one plane, as where objects were intersected or joined or a face was split.
  • Edges are hidden, never erased; the faces stay as they are. View › Hidden Geometry shows them.
  • A selected face brings its own edges.
  • Locked groups and components are left alone and counted.
  • One Undo, or Show hidden edges, brings them back.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
