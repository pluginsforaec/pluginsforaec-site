Smart Slab

Select the level faces of a floor and run Smart Slab: the slab's finished top is the face, its layers laid under it as built — a floor (finish, screed, reinforced concrete), a ground floor (finish, screed, insulation, concrete, blinding), a terrace roof (protection screed, membrane, insulation, sloping screed, concrete) or plain concrete. The box asks the type and the concrete's thickness (cm). Each layer is its own closed solid with its material, so Section Sheet hatches it by what it is; holes in the face stay holes; the face is hidden under the slab. One Undo.

Pictures and a short film of Smart Slab at work come here.

How to use it

  1. Select the level faces of the floor (a room's floor, a terrace).
  2. Click Smart Slab.
  3. In the box: the type and the concrete's thickness in cm.
  4. The slab is built under each face, its top at the face; the face is hidden.
  5. One Undo takes it back.

Good to know

  • Each layer is a closed solid with its material: Section Sheet hatches it.
  • Holes in the face (a stair's opening) stay open.
