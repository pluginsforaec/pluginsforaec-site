Line to Face

Raise lines into walls, Push/Pull style: click a line, move the mouse up or down, click or type the height. Lean the walls with the orange handle or a typed angle; a plan leans out, in or one way.

How to use it

  1. Start the tool; lines you selected before are raised at once.
  2. Otherwise click a line (Ctrl+click adds more, a click on a floor takes its outline), or press Enter for the selection.
  3. Move the mouse up or down and click at the height, or type the height and press Enter.
  4. To lean the walls, drag the orange handle on their top sideways, or type an angle.
  5. Click the next line, or double-click a line to raise it to the same height again.
  6. Press Esc to finish these walls, and Esc again to leave.

Keys

↑

→ / ←
Direction red (X) / green (Y); the same key again goes back to blue

Direction square to the line, level; the same key again goes back to blue

FREE: the wall goes where the mouse points; Alt again goes back to the axes
Shift (hold)
Locks the direction; a snapped point then gives only its height. While dragging the lean handle, frees it from 15° steps
Ctrl+click
Adds or removes a line from what will be raised
Enter
Raises what is selected; while raising, builds at the shown or remembered height
Double-click
Raises a line to the last height, direction and lean
Esc
Back to picking (selection kept), then done with the last walls, then leave

Good to know

  • A selected face gives its edges; a curve is raised whole and its walls read as one smooth surface.
  • The model's drawing axes are used for the directions.
  • Lean is limited to ±80°. On the card, LEAN chooses Out, In or One way for a plan; partitions stay upright.
  • RESULT on the card: Loose walls (default) or one group per build, named from the lines' tag and the height. Remembered.
  • When the view looks along the direction (Top view for walls), the mouse cannot set the height: type it or orbit.
  • Each build is one Undo; changing the last walls' height or lean joins that same Undo.
  • Closed groups and components are not entered: open them to raise their lines. Esc never clears your selection.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
