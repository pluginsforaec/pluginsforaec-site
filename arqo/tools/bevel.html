Bevel

Select a solid group or component (every outer edge) or open it and pick the edges, then run Bevel. Fillet rounds each edge with a radius in a number of flat facets whose edges are softened; Chamfer cuts it flat, set back the same on both faces. Edges meeting at a corner close the corner too. Edges at a hollow corner (and those whose cut would reach another part of the solid) are left and said; a size that would eat a face whole is refused. A copy of a shared component is separated first, so only the selected copy changes. One Undo.

Pictures and a short film of Bevel at work come here.

How to use it

  1. Select a solid group or component, or open it and pick edges.
  2. Click Bevel; in the box: Fillet or Chamfer, the size and (for a fillet) the segments.
  3. Every outer edge is done; corners close too.

Good to know

  • Edges at a hollow corner, and on an L or U those whose flat cut would reach the other arm, are left as they are.
  • A shared component: only the selected copy changes.
  • One Undo takes it back.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
