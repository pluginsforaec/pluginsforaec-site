Align+

Line objects up or space them evenly: Tab or a typed mode, a live preview, and hover an object to align to it.

Pictures and a short film of Align+ at work come here.

How to use it

  1. Select the groups, components, faces or edges to move, then start Align+ (Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp).
  2. Choose the axis with the arrows: right X (red), left Y (green), up Z (blue).
  3. Press Tab to step through low side, centre, high side, even spread and points.
  4. Hover any object to preview, then click it to align to it.
  5. Or click empty space to align the selection to its own outermost side.
  6. Points: click a source point and where it goes; a second pair turns it to fit, Enter only moves.

Keys

→ ← ↑
choose the axis: X red, Y green, Z blue (not in points mode)
Tab
next place to line up: low side, centre, high side, spread, points
Shift
points mode: also scale to fit the second pair (on / off)
Enter
points mode, after one pair: move only
Esc
points mode: clear the clicked points; otherwise leave the tool

Type in the measurements box

left
lines up the left sides (min X) of the selection at once; also right, front, back, top, bottom
cx
lines up the centres along X (cy, cz for Y and Z)
dx
spreads the objects at equal gaps along X (dy, dz for Y and Z)
points
switches to point-to-point (p works too)

Good to know

  • Sizes come from the geometry: text, dimensions and guides inside a group do not count.
  • Loose faces and edges joined together move as one body.
  • Spreading needs three or more objects: the first and last stay, the ones between move.
  • Aligning to empty space needs two or more objects; a single object is aligned by clicking another object.
  • Locked groups and components are skipped and counted.
  • If nothing would move, it says the selection is already aligned and changes nothing.
  • The last mode is remembered. One Undo removes the whole move.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
