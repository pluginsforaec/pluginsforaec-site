Find Gap

Find gaps and open edge ends and mark them; Connect / Heal closes them.

Pictures and a short film of Find Gap at work come here.

How to use it

  1. Select the edges to check, for example a plan outline that will not make a face.
  2. Click Find Gap (toolbar, or Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp › DOCTOR).
  3. Look at the marks: each gap is drawn with a ring at its middle, and the edges at the open ends become the selection.
  4. Read the message: gaps found and the smallest, gaps over the limit, and ends with no other end near.
  5. Run Connect/Heal to close the gaps, or raise the gap limit in Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp › Settings › Find Gap…

Good to know

  • Gap limit: 25 mm by default. Ends are paired nearest first, each end once, the same rule Connect/Heal uses.
  • Only gaps between two open ends count; an end that stops near a junction or an edge is not a gap.
  • Nothing is written to the model and there is no Undo step; the marks go away when the selection or the model changes.
  • With no open end found, your selection stays as it was.
  • Closed groups and components are not entered; ends in different contexts are not paired.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
