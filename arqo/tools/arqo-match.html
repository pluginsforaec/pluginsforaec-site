Tools · Move & scale

Match

Select two or more groups or components and run Match: in the box choose the model among them by name, and what the others take from it — its turn (each keeps its own place and scale), its size (each scaled about its own corner to the model's box), its material, its tag, its level (each moved up or down to the model's base). One Undo; the message says what they took.

Pictures and a short film of Match at work come here.

How to use it

  1. Select two or more groups or components.
  2. Click Match.
  3. In the box: the model (by name) and what the others take from it.
  4. They take it; the message says what.

Good to know

  • Turn keeps each one's own place and scale.
  • Size scales each about its own corner to the model's box.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
