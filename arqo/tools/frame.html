Frame

A frame along the outer outline of each selected face — inside it, outside it or centred on it, mitred at the corners, in a group of its own.

How to use it

  1. Select one or more faces and start Frame (toolbar, or Extensions › ARQO for SketchUp › GEOMETRY); or start it and click a face.
  2. Press Tab (or use Band on the card) to put the band Inside, Outside or Centred on the outline.
  3. Type the width, or width and depth, and press Enter to make the frames.
  4. Or click, press Enter, or use the Make button on the card to make them with the sizes shown.

Keys

Tab
band inside, outside or centred on the outline
Enter
make the frames
Esc
leave the tool

Type in the measurements box

50
width 50, in the model's units
50;30
width 50, depth 30
x;-30
depth only; a negative depth puts the frame behind the face

Good to know

  • The band runs along the face's outer outline and stands the depth out of the face's front; the face itself is not changed.
  • Corners are mitred; each segment of a curve becomes a straight piece.
  • A face too small for the width is left out and counted; if none fits, nothing is drawn.
  • The default section is 50 × 50 mm; width, depth and band side are remembered.
  • One group per frame, drawn in the open context; one Undo.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
