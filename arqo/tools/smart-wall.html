Smart Wall

Click the wall's path: each click adds a length of wall, the corners mitred. Ctrl picks the type: generic, exterior (render, insulation, masonry, plaster), interior (plaster, masonry, plaster), drywall (board, studs, board) or concrete; a layered wall has its outside on the right as you draw, a ring's outside outwards, each layer a group with its material. Tab puts the path on the wall's centre, left or right face; type a length, or the thickness and height ("250, 2700"; before the first click a number is the thickness, a layered wall's core taking up the change). Each wall run is a group on the tag ARQO Walls with its type, layers and sizes written on it, for Smart Door / Window.

How to use it

  1. Start Smart Wall; press Ctrl for the wall's type and Tab to put the path on the wall's centre, its left face or its right face.
  2. Before the first click, type the thickness, or the thickness and the height (250, 2700), if you need them.
  3. Click where the wall starts.
  4. Click each corner, or move the mouse the way the wall goes and type a length; the preview follows the mouse.
  5. Press Enter or double-click to end; click the first point to close the walls into a ring.

Good to know

  • Left and right are seen walking along the path: with the path as the left face, the wall lies to its right.
  • The walls are drawn in the plane of the first click; every point is brought to its height. A plain number is in the model's units, as in SketchUp's own boxes.
  • The types, outside first: exterior render 10, insulation 50, masonry 190, plaster 20 (270); interior plaster 15, masonry 100, plaster 15 (130); drywall board 12.5, studs 75, board 12.5 (100); concrete 200; generic one solid.
  • A layered wall's outside is on the right of the path as you draw it; in a ring it is always outwards. Each layer is a group inside the wall, named and painted (ARQO Render, Insulation, Masonry, Plaster, Plasterboard, Studs, Concrete).
  • Each wall run is one group on the tag ARQO Walls, named for its sizes, with its thickness, height, side and path written on it for Smart Door / Window.
  • Side, type, each type's thickness and the height are remembered (200 × 3000 mm at first). A path the thickness does not fit (a ring too small, a corner folding back) is refused and nothing is drawn.
  • Drawn in the open context; one Undo removes a wall run, and the tool stays for the next. Not yet: joining into walls already there (T joints).

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
