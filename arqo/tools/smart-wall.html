Smart Wall

Click the wall's path: each click adds a length of wall, the corners mitred; Tab puts the path on the wall's centre, left or right face; typed lengths, t200 for the thickness, h2800 for the height. Each wall run is a group on the tag ARQO Walls with its sizes written on it, for Smart Door / Window.
