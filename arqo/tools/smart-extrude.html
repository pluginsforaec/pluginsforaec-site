Smart Extrude

Select a path (connected edges, open or closed, flat or in 3D) and a profile face, or only the path and type a circle (diameter) or a rectangle (width × height). The profile is carried along the path square to it everywhere, without twisting; at every corner the section is mitred so it keeps its size round the bend. The result is one closed solid in a group of its own, its curved runs soft; the path and the face stay as they were. A face already standing at one end of the path, square to it, is swept from where it stands; a face elsewhere is brought to the path's start, centred. One Undo.

Pictures and a short film of Smart Extrude at work come here.

How to use it

  1. Select the path, and a profile face if you have one.
  2. Click Smart Extrude; without a face, choose circle or rectangle and type its size.
  3. The solid is made in a group of its own; the path and the face stay.

Good to know

  • A face standing at the path's end, square to it, is swept from where it stands — a handrail profile drawn off the path stays off it.
  • A closed path makes a ring with no ends.
  • A profile with a hole is not taken yet.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
