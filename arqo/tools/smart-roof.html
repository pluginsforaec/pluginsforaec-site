Smart Roof

Click round the outer line of the walls at their top (a click on the first corner closes it). It resolves the roof for any outline — a rectangle, an L, a T, a U: hips at the outer corners, valleys at the inner ones, ridges where the planes meet. Hipped: every side slopes; gable: the ends of the wings stand as gables; mono: the first side clicked is the low eaves. Then what a carpenter builds: rafters 5/10 @50, hip and valley rafters, a plate on the walls, purlins at most 2.2 m apart, posts on sole plates at most 3 m apart, collars and 45° braces; the tiles over it and the gables filled. Type the pitch (33%) or the eaves (600mm). The card checks the pitch against the tiles and says the ridge, the area and the tiles. One group on the tag ARQO Roof, one Undo.

How to use it

  1. Start Smart Roof. Press Tab for the form: Hipped (every side slopes), Gable (the ends of the wings stand as gables) or Mono (the first side you click is the low eaves). Choose the tiles on the card.
  2. Type the pitch (33%) or the eaves (600mm) if you need them.
  3. Click the corners of the walls' outer line at their top, one after another.
  4. The card shows the ridge's height, the roof's area, the tiles and the checks while you go.
  5. Click the first corner again (or press Enter) to close the outline: the roof is built.

Good to know

  • Any outline works — a rectangle, an L, a T, a U: hips form at the outer corners, valleys at the inner ones, ridges where the planes meet.
  • The structure: rafters 5/10 every 500, hip and valley rafters 8/16, a plate on the walls, purlins at most 2.2 m apart and a ridge purlin, posts 10/10 on sole plates at most 3 m apart (none on the walls), pairs of collars towards the eaves cut level with the rafters' top, and 45° braces along the purlins. Each member is a closed box, grouped by kind.
  • Tiles: Marseille, Mediterranean, Alaturka or concrete. The pitch is checked against them: no tiles under 20 %, an underlay needed under 30 % (Marseille).
  • One group on the tag ARQO Roof; one Undo removes it. The roof's plan is written on it.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
