Panel

A panel — its own object — on each selected face (holes, paint and texture kept) or from three points; a typed thickness, in front of, behind or centred on the face. The face stays as it was. One Undo.

How to use it

  1. Select the faces to clad, or select nothing to draw a panel from points.
  2. Start Panel and type a thickness, then press Enter.
  3. Press Tab (or use the card) to put the board in front of, behind or centred on the face.
  4. With faces selected: click anywhere, or press Enter, to make one panel on each face.
  5. With nothing selected: click two corners along the base, then click the height.

Keys

Tab
Switch the side: in front, behind, centred
Enter
With faces selected: make the panels
Shift
From the second point: hold to keep the direction
Arrows
From the second point: lock to an axis
Esc
Drop the points picked so far; again to leave the tool

Type in the measurements box

18mm
Thickness of 18 mm
18
Thickness of 18 in the model's units

Good to know

  • The selected face stays as it was; the panel is a copy of it in a new group, with holes, paint and texture kept.
  • Each panel is its own group, named after its thickness.
  • The three points must form a rectangle: two along the base, the third away from it.
  • Thickness and side are remembered for next time.
  • Panels are drawn in the context you have open. One Undo removes them all.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
