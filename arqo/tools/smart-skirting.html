Smart Skirting

Select the floor faces of rooms and run Smart Skirting: a skirting round each room against its walls and round each column standing in it, the corners mitred, nothing across a doorway (it stops wherever no wall stands right behind the floor's edge). Four kinds: white MDF 15 × 100, oak 15 × 80, a flush aluminium shadow line 10 × 60, marble 10 × 100. Each run is its own closed solid; one group a room on the tag ARQO Skirting; the message gives the length in metres. One Undo.

Pictures and a short film of Smart Skirting at work come here.

How to use it

  1. Select the floor faces of the rooms.
  2. Click Smart Skirting.
  3. In the box: the kind.
  4. Each room gets its skirting against its walls and round its columns, corners mitred.

Good to know

  • Where no wall stands right behind the floor's edge (a door, an open side) the skirting stops.
  • The message gives the length in metres for the order.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
