Smart Door / Window

Point at a Smart Wall: the opening follows the mouse, the card says how far it is from the wall's ends; a click cuts the hole through the wall, through every layer of a layered one, and sets the frame in it. Tab: door or window; Ctrl: the type, a door single, double, glazed, sliding or a lined opening, a window casement, double casement, fixed, sliding or top-hung. Type the sizes ("900, 2100"; a window also its sill: "1200, 1500, 900"), each type remembering its own. An opening never crosses a corner or another opening. The frames are components on the tag ARQO Openings, each part named and painted: lining, leaf, glass, handles, sill.

How to use it

  1. Start Smart Door / Window; Tab chooses door or window, Ctrl the family, the up and down arrows the type in it.
  2. Type the sizes if you need them: a door's width and height (900, 2100), a window's width, height and sill (1200, 1500, 900).
  3. Left and right arrows set how far it stands open (0–90°), Shift its hand: hinged left or right, opening in or out.
  4. Move the mouse over a wall: a Smart Wall, any group or component, or loose geometry. The opening follows it; the card says how far it is from the ends. Click to set it.
  5. To change one later, select it and start the tool: every change on the card applies to it at once.

Good to know

  • Doors: flush, grooved, vision panel, porthole, panelled (2, 4, 6, shaker, three rails), glazed (full, half, three lites, French, with a sidelight), double (with a transom too), barn, pocket and patio sliding doors, bifolds, arched ones and lined openings.
  • Windows: fixed (with glazing bars), casements (left, right, double, triple, with a fixed or a top light), tilt and turn, top-hung, bottom-hung, pivot, sliding (two, three panels, lift and slide), sash windows, round-top, fanlight, gothic, oculus, louvre, bathroom (frosted), ribbon and French windows.
  • Everything is ARQO's own: profiled linings and frames, bevelled architraves, a door stop, hinges, levers, beads and glazing bars, double glazing, a sloped sill and a window board, oak, painted and brushed-metal finishes we made ourselves. Arches are cut as arches.
  • In a layered wall the hole goes through every layer and its reveals take the finishes: render outside, plaster inside.
  • Component: select any door or window you have (3D Warehouse, a maker's catalogue, your own) before starting the tool; it is fitted into the wall, turned to run along it and centred in its depth.
  • Changing a placed opening draws its wall again from the wall's own data and cuts every opening in it again: no trace of the old hole. One Undo a change; the tool remembers each type's sizes.
  • Any wall: in a group or component the hole goes through the copy you point at (a shared component is made unique first, the other copies untouched); in loose geometry through the block. Only a Smart Wall is drawn again from its data, so elsewhere a placed opening keeps its size: its angle, hand and details still change.
  • Curved walls: a curved wall made as a group takes an opening across its facets, centred on the mouse; the wall stays one closed group with its name, tag and data. A curved face of loose geometry is refused: make it a group first.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
