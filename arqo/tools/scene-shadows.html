Scene Shadows

Shadows on or off, the hour, day and month, into every scene (or the current one) at once; each scene is updated to keep them and the scene shown before comes back. A scene update is not an Undo step in SketchUp (measured): to go back, run it again with the old values.

How to use it

  1. Open a model that has scenes.
  2. Run Scene Shadows; a box opens with your last values.
  3. Set Shadows on or off, then the hour, day and month.
  4. Choose all scenes or only the current one, and confirm.
  5. The scenes are updated and you return to the view you had.

Type in the measurements box

14
Hour field: 14:00
21
Day field: the 21st
6
Month field: June

Good to know

  • Hour must be a whole number from 0 to 23, day from 1 to 31, month from 1 to 12; otherwise the box stays open and says which field is wrong.
  • Each scene is updated only for its shadow settings.
  • The scene you are looking at shows the new shadows at once.
  • The values are remembered for next time.
  • Scene updates are not undo steps in SketchUp, so Undo does not take this back.

Always

  • One Undo takes the whole action back.
  • Shared components: you choose this copy or all copies.
  • The result is told in plain words; a refusal changes nothing.
