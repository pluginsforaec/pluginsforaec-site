SketchUp plugins for architects: the tasks that matter

SketchUp is quick for ideas and slow for the repetitive parts of a project. These are the tasks where a plugin saves an architect the most time, and the ARQO tools that do them.

Massing and form

Push/pull is where most SketchUp time goes. ARQOPush keeps SketchUp's push/pull and adds twelve modes: many faces at once, up to a face or an object, thicken, taper, offset, copy, symmetric, along an axis, repeat. Make Box+ draws a closed box from three points and a height; Smart Offset offsets with guided distances; Panel and Frame add boards and frames on faces.

Walls and stairs

Smart Wall draws walls along a path with a thickness and a height, the corners mitred. Smart Stair makes a straight flight between two levels in two clicks and checks it against the rules for risers and goings.

Cuts and sections

Smart Slice cuts geometry with a reference plane; Multi Slice makes many parallel cuts in one go, in equal parts or at a spacing. Divide+ splits edges, curves and faces into equal parts, previewed first.

Cleaning up drawings and imports

Imported plans are rarely ready to model on. Import DXF brings DXF geometry into a controlled context, Find Gap finds open ends, Connect / Heal closes gaps, Weld+ and Curve to Arc rebuild curves, Make Face+ fills closed outlines and Delete Overlap removes duplicates. The clean-up guide puts them in order.

Organising the model

Tag Tools assigns, isolates and cleans up tags from the selection. To Components+ turns groups into components, with alike groups sharing one definition. Smart Select and Filter Selection select by kind, tag, material and visibility.

Presenting it

Scene Shadows gives every scene the same shadows, Export Scenes saves every scene as an image in one click, and Clipping Camera finds what makes close-up views clip. Import Camera brings cameras in from another model.

One toolbar

All of these sit on one ARQO toolbar and work the same way: SketchUp's keys and inference, typed values, one Undo for every action, and a plain message after each run. Browse them all on the ARQO page or read each tool's guide in the documentation.
