Cleaning up imported CAD drawings in SketchUp

A CAD plan imported into SketchUp often has gaps, broken curves, overlapping lines and no faces. This is the order that turns it into a clean base to model on.

1. Bring it in on its own

Import the drawing into a group of its own, on its own tag, so nothing in it touches the model you already have. Import DXF does this for DXF files and reports what it brought in.

2. Find the gaps

Faces need closed outlines, and CAD lines often stop just short of each other. Find Gap marks every open end, so you see where the outline is broken before you try to make faces.

3. Close them

Connect / Heal joins edges that nearly meet and repairs small gaps. Run it on the selection, check the result, and take it back with one Undo if a gap was meant to be there.

4. Rebuild curves

Arcs often arrive as chains of short lines. Curve to Arc turns chains that lie on a circle back into real arcs and circles; Weld+ joins the remaining connected edges into curves, so they select and offset as one.

5. Remove duplicates

Overlapping lines split faces in unexpected places. Delete Overlap finds and removes duplicate geometry that lies on top of other geometry.

6. Make the faces

With the outlines closed, Make Face+ creates the faces from the closed planar edges, and Hide / Show Lines hides the seams between faces in one plane.

7. Model on it

Now the plan is ready for walls and volumes: draw walls on it with Smart Wall, or push its faces up with ARQOPush. Organise what you made with Tag Tools.
